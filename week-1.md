Build Upon the Rock

Helaman 5:12
And now, my sons, remember, remember that it is upon the rock of our Redeemer, who is Christ, the Son of God, that ye must build your foundation; that when the devil shall send forth his mighty winds, yea, his shafts in the whirlwind, yea, when all his hail and his mighty storm shall beat upon you, it shall have no power over you to drag you down to the gulf of misery and endless wo, because of the rock upon which ye are built, which is a sure foundation, a foundation whereon if men build they cannot fall.

2 Nephi 28:28
And in fine, wo unto all those who tremble, and are angry because of the truth of God! For behold, he that is built upon the rock receiveth it with gladness; and he that is built upon a sandy foundation trembleth lest he shall fall.

Ephesians 2:19-22
19 Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God;
20 And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;
21 In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord:
22 In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.

The scriptures that we were given to reflect on all talk about how important it is to have a foundation. And I particularly liked the description in Helaman 5, verse 12, where it talked about Christ being the rock that we must build on, and that when we build, we need to be able to withstand wind, whirlwind, hail, storms, and even floods that could wash us away and drag us into the gulf. 

It is important to have a strong and solid foundation. Think about walking on a sandy beach and how much effort it is to constantly shift your balance to remain steady. Running is difficult because the ground gives way rather than pushing against you so that you gain momentum. 

But a foundation is most important when it is connected to something. If you had only the foundation for your house, it wouldn't have much meaning. It is when you build on the foundation that you give meaning to it. The plan of salvation is founded on the atonement of Christ, and it gains meaning when the children of God build upon that atonement to Become who they are meant to be. Can you imagine building your home next to the foundation instead of on it?  The foundation does you no good, if you choose not to build on it.  But we are free to build our own dream home.  We are not all required to use the same blueprint.

So a foundation needs to be solid and something that we can connect to. It is easier to walk on a rock than it is on the sand, but if you don't have a grip on it, you can slip right off the rock. 

Modern computing is vast and complex.  Containers provide a way to isolate specific workloads that can be expanded or contracted as demand changes.  Doing all of that manually would be extremely difficult so a way was created to automate container management.  It required a foundation that would support many different workloads, configurations, and environments.  Kubernetes is that foundation. It provides flexibility that can be applied to many different circumstances by allowing configuration to be determined by those using it.  Instead of running in sand, you can run on asphalt.  It still requires work, but it is easier.

A Kubernetes cluster is like a self-healing Christmas tree that can fix a burnt-out bulb or a missing or broken ornament.  You can choose your own ornaments and lights and you describe exactly what you want your tree to look like.  Because of this detailed description, if something goes wrong the tree can fix itself.  You provide the blueprint for how you want the decorations placed and if the cat steals one of the ornaments or a light goes out, the tree will replace them.  Everyone has different ornaments and other decorations, but the tree can hold them all.  You can choose how tall the tree is, how many branches it has, what shape it is.  Whatever works for you.  The decorations are the Pods.  Everyone's are different.  You tell the tree what each one should look like so that if something goes wrong, the tree can fix it.

The foundation must be solid and strong to be worthwhile, but it must be built on to give it meaning.
